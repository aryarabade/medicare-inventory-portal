Medical Inventory Management System

Smartly manage medicines, monitor stock levels, track expiry dates, and streamline pharmacy operations with ease.

This system helps medical stores efficiently maintain inventory, avoid stock shortages, and keep track of medicine dispensing records.

Key Features of the Inventory Tracker

📦 Medicine Stock

Easily track available medicines and manage inventory levels in real time.

⚠ Expiry Alerts

Automatically identify medicines nearing expiry to prevent wastage.

💊 Dispense Tracking

Record medicines dispensed to patients and maintain transaction .

🔎 Smart Search

Quickly search medicines in stock and check their availability instantly.

How the Inventory System Works

1️⃣ Add Medicines

Admins can add medicine details including batch number, supplier, expiry date, and quantity.

2️⃣ Monitor Inventory

View stock availability and receive alerts when medicines are low or about to expire.

3️⃣ Dispense Medicines

Record medicines dispensed to patients while automatically updating inventory levels.
